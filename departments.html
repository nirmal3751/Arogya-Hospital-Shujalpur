Our Centres of Clinical Excellence

Arogya Hospital offers comprehensive medical specialties and advanced care tailored to your family's needs.

Cardiology (हृदय रोग)

Interventional specialist care for heart attacks, hypertension, angiography, and advanced heart procedures.

Neurology (स्नायु रोग)

Specialized treatment for migraine, stroke, epilepsy, paralysis, and complex spinal disorders.

Gastroenterology (पेट एवं लिवर)

Advanced diagnosis and treatment for acidity, IBS, Fatty Liver, Jaundice, and Endoscopy services.

General Surgery & O.T.

Modern Operation Theater facilities available for General and Laparoscopic surgical needs, led by our Founder.

Critical Care Unit (ICU)

24/7 dedicated and fully equipped Intensive Care Unit with ventilators and expert critical care staff.

Advanced Radiology & CT Scan

State-of-the-art CT Scan, X-ray, and portable diagnostic services for quick and accurate results.

Inpatient Wards & Rooms

Comfortable and clean General Wards and Private Rooms available for recovery and long-term care.

24/7 Pharmacy & Billing

In-house Medical Store available round-the-clock for prescription needs and cashless services.
