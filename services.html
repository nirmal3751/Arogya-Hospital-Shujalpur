Comprehensive Patient Care and Advanced Facilities

We are equipped with modern technology and infrastructure to ensure world-class healthcare is accessible right here in Shujalpur.

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

24/7 dedicated and fully equipped ICU with continuous monitoring, ventilators, and expert critical care staff.

CT Scan & Advanced Diagnostics

State-of-the-art CT Scan (Toshiba machine), X-ray, and portable diagnostic services for quick and accurate results.

24/7 Emergency & Trauma Care

Immediate, round-the-clock response team, trauma unit, and dedicated emergency beds available 365 days a year.

Modern Operation Theater

Fully equipped modular Operation Theater (O.T.) with advanced life support for General and Laparoscopic surgeries.

In-House Pharmacy & Cashless

24-hour Medical Store, prescription fulfillment, and coordination with major insurance providers for cashless treatment.

Comfortable Inpatient Wards

Clean and spacious General Wards, Private Rooms, and Semi-Private Rooms designed for patient comfort and recovery.
